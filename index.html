Site Outages and a Slower Holiday Week

Hey folks. Like the rest of the internet & other tech infrastructure, KDO has seen some outages in the past week or so, amounting to a few hours of downtime. Our time here together isn’t what I would call mission critical — reading the site an hour or two after you’d originally wanted to is not the worst thing in the world — but it’s still a bummer to have the site be unavailable. The reasons for the KDO downtime have been various, but that it occurred around the same time as some of these other outages isn’t, IMO, coincidental. Security researchers and developers armed with LLMs are going bananas finding exploits in a bunch of the software and infrastructure underpinning, well, everything. On the other side of the aisle, sysadmins are scrambling to fix all of these newly found exploits. But of course, poking holes in things is easier & faster than plugging them. LLMs can help with the patching,1 but it’s asymmetric warfare — being slow & careful with upgrades and patches isn’t a luxury you have when so many things are coming at you on a daily basis. Hopefully the situation won’t get too much worse before it gets better, but in the meantime, I’m thankful for everyone who is trying to keep these systems running . 🙏 Second thing: after a few months on the other side of the world, I’m spending some time with my family for Thanksgiving, and so the site will be a bit slower this week and very early next week. I’ve been pushing quite a bit over the past several weeks and it’s nice to take a bit of a break. And it’s probably hurting in other cases, with inexperienced teams rolling out LLM-coded “upgrades” that do more harm than good. ↩ Tags: kottke.org
